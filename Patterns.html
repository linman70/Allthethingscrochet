Free Crochet Patterns

Choose a project, gather your yarn and enjoy making something special. These patterns use US crochet terms. Finished size may vary with yarn, hook and individual tension.

Pattern 01

Classic Granny Square Blanket

BeginnerWorsted yarn5 mm hook

Make nine classic granny squares and join them into a cozy lap blanket. Choose three or four colors and arrange the squares in a 3-by-3 layout.

Finished size: approximately 27–30 inches square, depending on gauge and border.

Materials

  • Medium-weight (worsted) yarn in 3–4 colors, approximately 700–900 yards total
  • 5 mm crochet hook, yarn needle and scissors

Abbreviations (US terms)

ch = chain · sl st = slip stitch · dc = double crochet · sp = space

Make each granny square

  1. Round 1: Ch 4 and join with a sl st to form a ring. Ch 3 (counts as dc), work 2 dc into ring, ch 2. Repeat [3 dc into ring, ch 2] three times. Join to top of beginning ch-3.
  2. Round 2: Sl st across to a corner space. Ch 3, work 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in that same corner. Ch 1. Repeat [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] in each remaining corner, with ch 1 between corner groups. Join.
  3. Round 3 onward: Sl st to a corner. In each corner space work [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc]. In each side space between groups work 3 dc, with ch 1 between groups. Join each round and repeat until the square measures about 9 inches.
  4. Fasten off and weave in ends. Make 9 squares, all the same size.

Join and finish

  1. Arrange squares in a 3-by-3 layout.
  2. Join edges with a yarn needle and whip stitch, or crochet together with slip stitches.
  3. Work one round of single crochet evenly around the blanket, placing 3 sc in each corner. Join and weave in ends.
Tip: Make and measure one square before completing the other eight. Adjust hook size or round count to reach your desired size.
Pattern 02

Sweet Little Amigurumi Bunny

Confident beginnerDK yarn3 mm hook

A little stuffed bunny with a rounded body, floppy ears and embroidered features. Worked in continuous rounds from the top down.

Finished size: approximately 5–6 inches tall.

Materials

  • DK-weight yarn in a main color and a small amount of pink
  • 3 mm hook, polyester stuffing, stitch marker, yarn needle and scissors
  • Black embroidery thread for eyes

Abbreviations (US terms)

MR = magic ring · sc = single crochet · inc = 2 sc in one stitch · dec = invisible decrease · FO = fasten off

Head and body (one piece)

  1. Round 1: 6 sc in MR (6).
  2. Round 2: inc in each stitch around (12).
  3. Round 3: [sc, inc] around (18).
  4. Round 4: [2 sc, inc] around (24).
  5. Rounds 5–8: sc around (24).
  6. Round 9: [2 sc, dec] around (18).
  7. Round 10: [sc, dec] around (12). Begin stuffing the head.
  8. Round 11: inc in each stitch around (24).
  9. Rounds 12–16: sc around (24). Add stuffing as you go.
  10. Round 17: [2 sc, dec] around (18).
  11. Round 18: [sc, dec] around (12). Top up stuffing.
  12. Round 19: dec around (6). FO, close the opening and weave in the end.

Ears (make 2)

  1. Ch 10. Starting in the second ch from hook, work 2 sc in each of the next 2 ch, sc in each of the next 5 ch, then 3 sc in the last ch.
  2. Working along the opposite side of the chain, sc in the next 5 ch and 2 sc in each of the last 2 ch. Sl st to first sc. FO, leaving a long sewing tail.
  3. Flatten each ear gently and sew to the top of the head.

Face and finishing

  1. With black embroidery thread, stitch two eyes onto the head, leaving about five stitches between them. Embroider a small pink nose and short mouth.
  2. Shape the ears and gently roll the body to even out the stuffing.
  3. Weave in all remaining ends securely.
Safety: For toys intended for babies or children under 3, embroider features instead of using safety eyes. Secure all parts firmly and follow applicable toy-safety guidance.
Pattern 03

Cozy Everyday Infinity Scarf

BeginnerBulky / weight 5 yarn6 mm hook

A simple ribbed rectangle joined at the short ends to make a warm loop scarf. Choose lavender, periwinkle or your favorite shade.

Finished size: approximately 8 inches wide by 60 inches long before joining.

Materials

  • Bulky (weight 5) yarn, approximately 250–300 yards
  • 6 mm crochet hook, yarn needle and scissors

Abbreviations (US terms)

ch = chain · hdc = half double crochet · st = stitch · blo = back loop only

Scarf instructions

  1. Foundation: Ch 25. This makes a scarf approximately 8 inches wide. Add or subtract chains in multiples of 2 to change the width.
  2. Row 1: Hdc in the third chain from hook and in each chain across. Ch 2 and turn.
  3. Row 2: Hdc in the back loop only of each stitch across. Ch 2 and turn.
  4. Repeat: Repeat Row 2 until the piece measures about 60 inches long, or your preferred length.
  5. Join: Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Bring short ends together without twisting the scarf. Sew the ends together with a yarn needle, matching each stitch. Weave in ends.
Tip: Drape the rectangle around your neck before joining to check the loop length. Decide whether you want a twisted or untwisted loop before sewing.
